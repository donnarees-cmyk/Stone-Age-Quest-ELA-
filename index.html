🔥 Stone Age Quest

Travel from a moving camp to an early farming village. Collect five knowledge badges in about 30 minutes!

Your expedition briefing

Plan for about 30 minutes. Read five field notes, solve 10 challenges, and write one short campfire story. This is a learning game: you can correct an answer after reading the feedback.

Suggested pace: briefing 2 minutes; missions 1–4 about 3 minutes each; campfire story and revision 13 minutes; completion report 3 minutes. Work at a steady pace and use the passages for help.

The characters and scenes are invented. The history reflects broad patterns; people in different places changed at different times. Not everyone lived in a cave, and humans did not live with non-avian dinosaurs.

Work independently. Use the passages for evidence. Progress saves on this browser when storage is available; on a shared device, start a new expedition before another student uses it.
